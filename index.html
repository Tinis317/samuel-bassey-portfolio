We-Ride: Saturday 9 PM Surge Pricing Analysis (Delhi)

Why does We-Ride charge 1.61x surge on Saturday at 9 PM in Delhi? A supply-demand investigation.

Key finding: Traffic is NOT the villain. Low traffic 1.42x vs Jam 1.45x = only 2% impact.
Real cause: Driver shortage. 10 drivers = 1.71x, 80 drivers = 1.31x. Demand-Supply Ratio 14.5x.
Recommendation: Add 35 to 65 drivers, Cost Rs 4500/Sat, ROI 40x.

Tools: Excel | Python | PowerPoint
